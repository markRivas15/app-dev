# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: The Book of Life

> A love story, a bet between gods, and a celebration of Día de los Muertos.

---

## Overview

**The Book of Life** is a *2014 animated fantasy-comedy film* directed by **Jorge R. Gutierrez** and produced by **Guillermo del Toro**.

## Why I Love It

1. The colorful, wooden-puppet-inspired animation style
2. The music and the way it blends humor with heart
3. Its message about family, courage, and being true to yourself

## Main Characters

| Character | Role |
| --------- | ---- |
| Manolo    | A musician torn between family expectations and his dream |
| María     | A strong, independent childhood friend |
| Joaquín   | A celebrated hero and Manolo's rival |
| La Muerte | Ruler of the Land of the Remembered |
| Xibalba   | Ruler of the Land of the Forgotten |

## Themes

- Family and remembrance
- Following your own path
- Love and friendship
- Mexican culture and traditions
